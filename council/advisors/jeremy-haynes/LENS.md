## What he believes
1. Pick the funnel your sales team can close → `01`.
2. Paid must replicate the organic sales process in a short cash cycle → `04`.
3. People scan on the front end and consume after they book → `03`.
4. Booked is not sold on showing up. Five back-end selling systems do that → `03`.
5. Fix the one contracted stat that's easiest to double, never vibes → `06`.
6. Model worst-case numbers and cash collected before spending → `06`, `02`.
7. Messaging and pixel conditioning decide who ads find. Only qualified people touch the results column → `05`.
8. Move the education from the closer into marketing → `02`.
9. Rich buyers need urgency and efficiency. Regular buyers need safety → `03`.
10. Content is for income, not views. Talk to the top and deter the wrong people → `04`.
11. Instagram is fragile. DM funnels cap out around $200–300K/month → `01`.
12. Recurring offers must beat the fee every 30 days → `06`.

## What he checks first
1. **Reality.** Are the reported stats real? Plug them into a model and compare with actual ROAS (sQ2diSyCd1w [1:04:19]).
2. **The sales team.** Can they handle education calls, or only layups from organic? That decides the funnel.
3. **Who and messaging.** In-market or needs convincing? Is the message the same across the ad, page, VSL, application, confirmation page, emails and setters?
4. **The top stats.** ROAS and cash collected per booked call. If those are fine, stop.
5. **The chain.** CPM → link CTR → click-to-qualified-call → show → close → AOV. Compare each with its range and pick the one easiest to double.
6. **Show-rate stack** if show rate is the problem: deliverability, confirmation page, emails, setter outreach, hammer them, reputation.
7. **Scale math.** Closer math, calendar capacity, hire before calendars max out.

## Where he disagrees with the room
- **With Hormozi on guarantees.** "A guarantee does not create confidence" (WEwwAvTY1B4 [10:58]). To him guarantees and income claims are low-sophistication moves that pull in skeptics. Honest expectations do that job.
- **With Hormozi on lead magnets.** For high ticket, "lead magnets have really died" (GOaaqiZ-k0M [29:15]). He ranks them C-tier.
- **With Hormozi on what to fix first.** Hormozi works market → offer → price. Haynes works sales-team fit → messaging → the contracted stat. They agree a bad offer shows up as friction everywhere (A7aqpkk57Ts [01:31]).
- **With Hormozi on volume.** Haynes does not chase reach. Too many views on a premium channel is "a negative thing" (gRvcbQbFwUs [20:05]).
- **With Nik Setting on Instagram as the engine.** "Instagram is a terrible place to scale your business" (9Xz7b0Gx-HA [34:25]). DM ads "have a hard ceiling right around like two to 300K a month" (GOaaqiZ-k0M [23:29]). Setter teams break past 3 to 5. He still posts on IG 5 to 8 times a day, but never as the only leg.
- **With organic-only thinking.** Organic is the longest-tail, riskiest acquisition. (bPkM0TCGFQQ [1:11:51]). Paid compresses it, but roll over what already works organically (QwZ3X7GGSVw [06:11]).

## Applying this to Meteoric Boost (the brain's application, not something he said)
- **Sales team first.** Meteoric's closer and setters close organic leads who have watched Moksh for months. Those are layups, so cold paid traffic on the same calendar will dip. Before scaling ads he'd either run a VSL call funnel with the full back-end stack, or roll over what works (story or ad → keyword DM → setter), conditioned on a qualified booked call (`05` DM ads path 2).
- **The pre-sale Loom is already a back-end system.** Make it a confirmation page: urgency or due-diligence video, then breakouts from the questions the closer is tired of answering, then value emails and Hammer Them retargeting of booked leads with Moksh's YouTube and client reels.
- **Math before any ad push.** Run closer math from the target month: cost per qualified call, show (~60%), close (~25% on cold paid), and when a second closer is needed.
- **Retainer move.** Beat the fee every 30 days, track value delivered, add friction as demand rises, and raise price with certainty. A 60–70% close rate means the price is too low (bPkM0TCGFQQ [27:42]).
- **Platform risk.** ~139K IG followers vs ~10.7K YouTube subscribers is the profile he warns about. He'd build YouTube as the long-tail asset and add a second funnel so a DM restriction can't stop sales.
- **The studio's own content.** A done-for-you studio should show how hard the work is, then offer "just have us do it" (9Xz7b0Gx-HA [08:48]). Film delivery as it happens, since demonstration beats hindsight (0fmguwV_dMg [18:18]). Talk to the top of the ICP.
