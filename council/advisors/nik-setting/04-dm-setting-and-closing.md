# 04 — DM setting, closing and the sales team

**When to open this file:** "our setter isn't booking", "show rates are low", "how do we close in DMs?", "do we need a closer?", "what KPIs for setters?", "how do I hire a setter?", "what happens between booking and the call?".
- He began as a setter and setter trainer (2022), so this is where his oldest material sits. Older advice is marked **[older view]** where newer videos contradict it.

### Booking rate before lead flow (UNNAMED; the $30K→$100K constraint)
**When to use:** founder at $30–100K/month asking for more leads.
**The idea:** Once the offer is validated, more lead flow isn't the fix; conversation volume and booking rate are. Warm outbound to existing followers, a setter from the first sale, and fixing the opener and the "Calendly link sent → booked" step.
**How to run it:**
1. Hire a setter "the moment you make your first sale" (0I_cEtloYb4 [07:39]).
2. Warm outbound to followers and engagers daily, within account limits.
3. Fix the two common mistakes: the opener, and link-sent → booked (0I_cEtloYb4 [03:05]).
**The numbers (medians he says came from ~30 clients at $30–50K/month, Oct 2026, 0I_cEtloYb4 [04:51–06:56]):** reel target 10,000 views; ~1% profile visits per view; 3% of profile visits convert inbound; warm outbound median 522 sent; clients send 20–25/day, "You need to send 80 to 100"; ~20% reply; 40% qualified; 50% of qualified booked; 75% show; 25% close standard, 25–40% with better pre-selling. Account limits: 80–100 messages/day on a ~2-year-old account, 40–50 on a 1-year-old one (0I_cEtloYb4 [02:23–02:44]). $100K is "extremely doable when you have about 2,000 3,000 followers" with very good setting (0I_cEtloYb4 [10:43]).
**Mistakes he calls out:** "more lead flow, if I can call it that way, is not the solution" (0I_cEtloYb4 [01:22]); a founder doing outreach himself (F10-qvBBNVE [01:00], 2022).
**Ask first:** How many conversations did the setter start yesterday, and how many links were sent vs booked?
**Source:** 0I_cEtloYb4 [01:00–10:43]

### Setter scripts and DM rules (UNNAMED; his phrasing varies)
**When to use:** writing or auditing setter scripts.
**The idea:** The setter's job is to start a curious conversation, qualify, educate with assets and book, not to sell. Every message acknowledges, answers, then asks a follow-up question.
**How to run it:**
1. Opener: "Yo" for curiosity (eGWeRi6siNQ [27:57]); inbound: "Yo, what got you interested to reach out?" which puts them "into a state of buying" (X7pn2j2tK9M [21:00–22:02]).
2. Either/or questions instead of open ones: "is the current bottleneck… the clarity on how to start it or is it the lead generation?" (MyeU41kG1SY [45:38–46:19]). His flow: starting or scaling? → volume or quality of leads? → three months or a year plus? (X7pn2j2tK9M [26:14–27:16]).
3. Bridge: "is that also the reason of why you reached out to me because you were looking for help" (X7pn2j2tK9M [28:16]).
4. Send an asset (pain-matched YouTube video) before asking for a call; wait for their follow-up question; then propose a time (X7pn2j2tK9M [28:56–29:57]).
5. Soft booking line: "if it makes sense for you we could potentially book in a call… let me know"; after yes, stop selling and ask time zone (8eWefcr7UVo [23:56–27:26], 2022).
6. 2022 rules still consistent with later advice: reply as soon as you open; build on the positive half of a reply; answer only the question asked; voice notes carry emotion; no "bro" in B2B; never ask budget bluntly, ask what they do now and why they want it (8eWefcr7UVo [02:23–13:09]).
**The numbers:** either/or questions took response from ~5% to ~20% (MyeU41kG1SY [45:38]); his flow gets ~60% response (X7pn2j2tK9M [26:14]); assets per conversation: under 50 conversations/day 0–1, 50–100 → 1–2, 100+ → 3 (X7pn2j2tK9M [02:45–03:26]). Outbound benchmarks: 15–25% positive response, 70–80% show, 15–25% close (X7pn2j2tK9M [22:44]).
**Mistakes he calls out:** "you don't sell them you just educate them and book them" (8eWefcr7UVo [00:21]); new followers get a YouTube video, not a call; old followers who engaged before are "the most qualified" to book (MyeU41kG1SY [50:25]).
**Ask first:** Show me the last ten setter conversations, start to finish.
**Source:** 8eWefcr7UVo, X7pn2j2tK9M [00:41–30:37], MyeU41kG1SY [44:35–51:46]

### Setter KPIs and management (UNNAMED)
**When to use:** setting targets, weekly reviews.
**The idea:** Optimise for qualified bookings, not bookings. Track every step; manage the person, not the step.
**How to run it:**
1. Daily volume: 50 new-follower DMs + 50 old-follower DMs + 50 follow-ups (MyeU41kG1SY [50:05–51:46], Sep 2025); or 100% of new followers within an hour plus 100 new conversations and 50 follow-ups a day (X7pn2j2tK9M [00:41–01:25], Aug 2025).
2. Track inbound DMs, warm outbound, active conversations, Calendly links sent, booked, show, close, and show rate per setter (MyeU41kG1SY [47:40–48:42]; YOuvbqU8liU [1:26:05–1:26:47]).
3. Work the "from ads" inbox folder; keep Primary personal (MyeU41kG1SY [46:39]).
4. Weekly KPI call (MyeU41kG1SY [49:45]); daily EOD Loom of 1–3 minutes with pain points; review calls daily in week 1, tapering to weekly by week 4 (F10-qvBBNVE [10:39]; whALnXkY3Zc [07:34]).
**The numbers:** a setter with 40% show vs 80% is "booking calls in too quick" (MyeU41kG1SY [48:42]); adding a DM setter to a webinar business "will will increase your revenue by 25%" (his estimate, YOuvbqU8liU [1:25:02]); "you can rather do 100 qualified outreaches than 200 unqualified outreaches" (whALnXkY3Zc [17:16]).
**Mistakes he calls out:** "nobody cares about appointments", optimise for qualified booking (whALnXkY3Zc [15:51]); inconsistent daily outreach that starves the setter (F10-qvBBNVE [05:08]).
**Source:** MyeU41kG1SY [44:35–51:46], X7pn2j2tK9M [00:41–03:46], YOuvbqU8liU [1:25:02–1:26:47], F10-qvBBNVE

### Hiring setters (UNNAMED)
**When to use:** "who should we hire?", "setters keep quitting".
**The idea:** Hire on internal state, not skill. The ideal setter wants remote income but doesn't want to build their own thing; the role is repetitive "just like an editing role". Record every training so a replacement onboards without you.
**How to run it:**
1. Screen out anyone who says they want to build their own $10K/month business (0I_cEtloYb4 [08:41–09:21]).
2. Train product knowledge (offer stack, how the founder pitches), technical setting, structure, tracking (0I_cEtloYb4 [09:21–10:01]).
3. Record setter SOPs "Day one to day 14", one hour each (t6jzUsmeUTU [53:32]); "An SOP is recording a moment" (CAxLCrjz5Ss [16:20]).
4. Hire from your own students or network first (7xAo-xh1DwQ [14:19]); judge natural talent and how they think (whALnXkY3Zc [12:04–13:27]).
**The numbers:** his setter (captioned Fabio) ~1.5 years vs most setters leaving within 3–6 weeks (0I_cEtloYb4 [08:21]); 130 closer applicants from one post (QKnBoqLD2Cc [21:44]). Comp: setter 5%, closer 10% (7xAo-xh1DwQ [15:00–15:21]); a sales manager at 4% is mentioned in the same consult, speaker unclear (7xAo-xh1DwQ [10:35]).
**[older view]:** 2023 "growth specialists" on 5–7.5% commission and offshore VAs at ~$400/month doing outreach (YKOqvhF2tT8; whALnXkY3Zc [03:47–04:28]). By 2025 the team is one setter, one VA, a closer/ops person and content (8t92OBM83BA [02:22]).
**Source:** 0I_cEtloYb4 [07:39–10:01], t6jzUsmeUTU [53:32], CAxLCrjz5Ss [16:20–17:43], whALnXkY3Zc

### DM closing (UNNAMED; "90% of the work is pre-work")
**When to use:** "can we close without calls?".
**The idea:** DM closes work because the content already did the selling: by the time someone messages, they're problem-aware, solution-aware and clear on the roadmap. Then you just send the payment link. It only pays at high ticket and with a pre-sold audience.
**How to run it:** quality at the top (niche content, pain hooks) → media funnel (long-form, stories, lead magnet, retargeting) → bottom (DM close, setter + call, or call close) (xTUK3UWMD9A [03:31–10:38]).
**The numbers:** "the reason why DM Clauses work is because 90% of the work is rework" (caption for pre-work; xTUK3UWMD9A [03:09]); about 50% of $14K sales PIF in DMs (MyeU41kG1SY [53:49]); first DM close came from someone who watched his YouTube for two weeks (Tr2WIG6-1VU [12:29]); one buyer paid $10K in DMs after ~1.5 weeks, "one of the fastest" (7xAo-xh1DwQ [07:29]); 60% of sales through calls, 40% DM closes (t6jzUsmeUTU [18:40], Oct 2025).
**Limit:** for a ~$4K offer "It's not worth it for you"; DM closing is for very high ticket (YOuvbqU8liU [1:16:26]).
**Mistakes he calls out:** two-hour sales calls and second calls for $2–3K offers (xTUK3UWMD9A [00:42–01:04]; 17Oc1LRuRKI [13:48]).
**Ask first:** How long has a typical buyer been watching before they DM?
**Source:** xTUK3UWMD9A, 17Oc1LRuRKI [11:24–12:05], MyeU41kG1SY [53:49–57:39]

### Calls as "onboarding calls" and the pre-call video (UNNAMED)
**When to use:** sales process design; low show or close rates.
**The idea:** He doesn't call them sales calls; buyers arrive ready, so the call confirms and onboards. The gap nobody fills is between booking and the call: fill it with a pre-call video and proof.
**How to run it:**
1. Between booking and call, send a pre-call video (~8 minutes in one consult) and a testimonial or deliverables doc (eJItxb8Hs-E [31:29–31:50]; X7pn2j2tK9M [03:46]; Tr2WIG6-1VU [15:17]).
2. If the founder isn't on calls, make a pre-call video where the founder praises the closer, so buyers stop asking for the founder (jgpVvH4rTfI [48:09]).
3. On price: ask "What is it costing you right now?" and "What happens if nothing changes in the next 12 months?"; once they say their number, yours is smaller (Tr2WIG6-1VU [11:06–11:26]).
4. Upsell on the gap, never on the past: "you want to skill 100K month right now you don't know how" (caption "skill" = scale; xTUK3UWMD9A [16:55]).
**The numbers:** 10–30 minute calls for $10K+ (8t92OBM83BA [07:55]; xTUK3UWMD9A [01:45]); closes "above 50%" within 30 minutes after the asset funnel (17Oc1LRuRKI [05:31]); show ~80–82% (wY5TWeKaJow [07:55]; 8t92OBM83BA [07:13]).
**Show-rate levers:** in 2022 he said rapport is "the only way" (F10-qvBBNVE [03:25]); by 2025 it's Calendly + email follow-ups + setter-sent value and testimonial assets (8t92OBM83BA [07:13]). Newer view: systems plus proof.
**Source:** b5urvngM8wU [05:30], eJItxb8Hs-E [30:49–32:52], Tr2WIG6-1VU [11:06–15:38], 8t92OBM83BA [07:13–07:55]

### Closers: what they're for (UNNAMED)
**When to use:** hiring or managing closers.
**The idea:** A closer's value is the process around the close: follow-up, pre-call, tracking, rewatching calls. If marketing does 80%, close rates make closing look easy.
**The numbers:** closer Herman on ~2 calls/day (~60/month) earning $10K+/month (MyeU41kG1SY [53:49–55:12]); later ~30 calls/month, ~10 closes, all PIF, ~80% show (X7pn2j2tK9M [31:58]); don't restrict closer availability unless they take ~40 calls/week or you miss "a $20,000 payment" (jgpVvH4rTfI [1:46:24]). Co-host on tracking: cash per booked call ÷ 10 = the closer's effective hourly rate on 10% commission (acqxhHB8a5g [29:53–30:13]).
**Founder calendar:** batch all sales and client calls into Monday–Tuesday, max ~20 calls (jgpVvH4rTfI [1:43:38–1:45:43], 2026); an older version blocks 9am–1pm daily for sales calls (t6jzUsmeUTU [51:28], 2025). Newer view: batch.
**Source:** acqxhHB8a5g [30:33–32:17], MyeU41kG1SY [53:49], jgpVvH4rTfI [1:43:38–1:48:28]

### Onboarding attribution form (UNNAMED)
**When to use:** every new client.
**The idea:** Ask every client why they paid and where they first found you, then make more of what converted the best clients.
**How to run it:** questions: name; first touch point (referral, YouTube, IG ad, reel, team member); "why did you choose me? Why me above anybody else?"; "how converted were you before the sales call?"; a co-host adds "what would have made you convert quicker?" (CAxLCrjz5Ss [23:51–24:31]; njWXZNfCm28 [17:14–17:55]). After ~20 clients, compare what the 10 best and 10 worst have in common (njWXZNfCm28 [17:55]).
**Source:** njWXZNfCm28 [17:14–17:55], CAxLCrjz5Ss [23:51–24:31], t6jzUsmeUTU [45:13–45:55], MyeU41kG1SY [17:01]
