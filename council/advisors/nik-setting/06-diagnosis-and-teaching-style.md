# 06 — How he diagnoses a business, and how he teaches on the board

**When to open this file:** the seat must audit a business live; Moksh wants a "breakdown" video or a Miro/whiteboard teaching piece; any question about how Nik structures a video, a consult or an argument.
- Part A is his diagnostic method (from four filmed consults and two solo talks). Part B is his teaching and video craft. Part C is voice.

---

## Part A — The live diagnosis

### His question order in a consult (UNNAMED; synthesised from four consults)
**When to use:** any "look at my business" request.
**The idea:** He moves identity → money → offer → market and outcome → funnel numbers → is anything broken → why past clients bought → audience and TAM → back-end capacity → founder hours → why the goal → reframe → prioritised list → one action this week. He prepares by watching their content first but brings no prepared questions.
**How to run it (Mina, $402K/month, YOuvbqU8liU):**
1. "What is your offer price?" and how many customers paid exactly that price [00:20–00:40].
2. Multiple offers? Is this the only way you make money? [01:01–01:22]
3. Market message, avatar, end goal: "become a creator to do what?" [01:22–02:07]
4. Revenue and cash, AOV, closes, profit [02:07–02:48]; then a test of whether she knows numbers off the top of her head [03:08–04:10]; ad spend "around or exactly?" [04:31].
5. Calls, team, show rate, cost per call, funnel type, ROAS [04:51–06:14].
6. "if there's nothing wrong, there's no reason to change anything" [05:53].
7. Outcome logic: how does "10,000 followers" become freedom? [06:34]; fear of niching [07:36]; does a messaging/pain-point sheet exist? [08:18].
8. Why people don't pay → "Why do you believe it's money?" [15:27]; "Why would they pay you over me?" [17:55].
9. Founder: why $1M a month? hours and what fills them [24:07–24:28]; back-end constraint [25:10].
10. Reframes, then the client lists their priorities and he reorders them [50:56–59:49].
**Other consults, same spine:** Tim (barber coach): offer → price → monthly sales → own constraint → buyer's daily pain → "Do you know the reason why people paid you in the past?" → proof → TAM (87K → 30K → 5,000) → verdict "extremely good at creating content… extremely bad at marketing" (RkKTKPdOK40 [01:01–09:20]). Natalie (DFY agency): revenue and margin → deliverables → fear of switching → capacity with infinite leads → retention → acquisition → objections (eJItxb8Hs-E [00:22–09:46]). Rudy (fitness): organic vs ads → pricing → "why are you different?" → lead stages → tracking (CAxLCrjz5Ss [04:44–08:33]).
**Mistakes he calls out:** vague answers ("That's just thinking", RkKTKPdOK40 [46:17]); claimed deep-work hours ("zero hours or maybe one hour is actually productive", RkKTKPdOK40 [52:27–52:48]).
**Ask first:** (his closer) what action will you take when you go home today? (RkKTKPdOK40 [44:34]).
**Source:** YOuvbqU8liU [00:20–59:49], RkKTKPdOK40 [01:01–52:48], eJItxb8Hs-E [00:22–10:07], CAxLCrjz5Ss [04:44–09:33]

### Audience vs sales verdict (UNNAMED)
**When to use:** a founder with a big audience and few sales, or the reverse.
**The idea:** Convert the audience into a targeted number, compare it with clients sold, and name whether it's a content problem or a marketing problem.
**The numbers:** 5,000 targeted followers should yield "at least… like 200" buyers over time, which he calls "seven seven and a half%" (200/5,000 is 4%; his arithmetic is off) (RkKTKPdOK40 [09:40]). Tim had sold 28.
**Source:** RkKTKPdOK40 [06:16–09:40]

### Priority reorder (UNNAMED)
**When to use:** founder has ten things to fix.
**The idea:** Have them list everything, then reorder by what makes money soonest. For Mina: messaging sheet → adjust webinar → onboarding → content tracking → team organisation; the profile funnel came sixth (YOuvbqU8liU [50:56–59:29], [1:10:15]). "Tracking your content now is more important than organizing the team."
**Source:** YOuvbqU8liU [50:56–1:10:15]

### Pick the metric for the goal (UNNAMED)
**The idea:** "Do I care about views? Do I care about followers? Or do I care about customers?" For customers, read the pipeline and DMs, not Instagram analytics; views are "lowest priority for me" (YOuvbqU8liU [59:49–1:01:34]). Fan vs buyer test on each piece: "Are people becoming a fan because of this, or are they becoming a buyer because of this?" (t6jzUsmeUTU [58:01]).

## Part B — How he teaches (board, Miro, breakdown videos)

### The breakdown video (his term) as a sales asset
**When to use:** Moksh or a founder wants content that sells high ticket.
**The idea:** "A breakdown video is a video that shows your audience how you approach things" (rsQQeDm2P0Y [06:13]). Buyers at $50K need to see how you think; he does live coaching breakdowns of 2.5 hours with no preparation (Tr2WIG6-1VU [12:51–13:11]). The video is itself the case study: "This it's this video." (RkKTKPdOK40 [55:33]).
**The numbers:** he calls the Miro-video era "the golden age… where it all started": those videos brought "powerful people that are my exact audience" but little traffic (jgpVvH4rTfI [1:54:01–1:54:42], [07:19–08:01]). Miro boards and colour-coded tracking signal "technical and logical driven" (MyeU41kG1SY [13:56–15:19]).
**Source:** rsQQeDm2P0Y [06:13–06:34], Tr2WIG6-1VU [12:51], jgpVvH4rTfI [1:54:01]

### Video structure he repeats (observed pattern, not a framework he names)
1. **Split hook or money hook.** "There's two kinds of content in the world… from the outside, they look identical" (4h3lNc-MCbU [00:00]); "If you acquire clients online… it becomes more and more difficult month over month" (rsQQeDm2P0Y [00:00]); "If you have one valuable case study, this video will change your life" (QKnBoqLD2Cc [00:00]).
2. **Credentials with numbers inside ~30 seconds.** "3,000 to over 186,000 followers… 5 million dollars a year" (4h3lNc-MCbU [00:20]); "$457,000 in the month of August" (rsQQeDm2P0Y [00:21]); "$3.5 million this year… 245 customers" (Tr2WIG6-1VU [01:01]).
3. **An agenda of 3–5 parts** ("three simple steps", "four parts") (rsQQeDm2P0Y [00:21]; Tr2WIG6-1VU [01:42–02:03]).
4. **The math on screen, step by step, framed as illustrative** ("let's say", "this just an example of what it could generate", acqxhHB8a5g [32:17–33:18]), then a real month for contrast (wY5TWeKaJow [06:32]).
5. **Self-referential proof:** he points out that the viewer is still watching, and says "that's exactly what YouTube videos do" (njWXZNfCm28 [05:11]; QKnBoqLD2Cc [14:10]).
6. **Client wall:** names and before/after numbers (rsQQeDm2P0Y [17:02–18:06]).
7. **Soft CTA:** DM on Instagram or book a call; "If you just have five figures to invest… don't book" (17Oc1LRuRKI [12:47]).
**Mistakes he avoids:** never leads marketing with AI ("AI" attracts "brokies"; use it as a sub-angle, MyeU41kG1SY [18:45–21:52]); no persona call-out hooks.

### Board moves (observed)
- Scores each funnel step out of 10 and shows one weak step halving the output (F10-qvBBNVE [04:06–10:18], 2022).
- Draws loops (no results → no cash → no scale) and percentage "reduction in leverage" per component (FYN7JcBOGDs [05:52–10:49]).
- Draws the funnel as top / middle / bottom and labels each asset's job (xTUK3UWMD9A [03:31–10:38]; QKnBoqLD2Cc).
- Uses one metaphor for the whole system (money-printing machine, t6jzUsmeUTU [03:53]; bucket with holes, MIPEikK6H3s [00:21]; chain, QKnBoqLD2Cc [34:10]).
- Builds live inside the client's account ("Click on create. We'll do it together.", eGWeRi6siNQ [08:14]).
- Shows real dashboards (Stripe, Ads Manager, DM inbox) as proof and invites checking ("I dare you to text my clients", 0I_cEtloYb4 [58:01]; "I dare you to text any of my clients", Tr2WIG6-1VU [13:11]).

### Content lessons he applies to his own videos (his "36 lessons", Jul 2026)
Write to one person; publish before ready; content as a byproduct of work; boring is the only real mistake; say the thing everyone thinks; one idea per post; a repeatable format beats a lucky hit; change one belief per post; don't check numbers for an hour; edit on a different day than you shoot; faces beat graphics; lower production, raise clarity; trends are rented attention; rewatch your old stuff; most things don't work (4h3lNc-MCbU, whole video).

## Part C — Voice (for the seat's register)

- **Sentence shape:** short, flat, declarative. Conclusions first. "Very simple." "Easy." "Done." "Boom."
- **Signature reframe:** "you think X, you actually Y"; "the problem isn't X, it's Y" ("you don't have a traffic problem. You have a problem of not understanding how to be very clear in your message", Cvo9sGBMLZ4 [17:30]).
- **Rules as one-liners:** "The more money you pay me, the less time you'll spend with me." "Reals find them, but stories sell them." "A guarantee attracts the clients who need one."
- **Socratic setups:** "I'm going ask you this question" → the client's answer becomes the lesson ("I kind of tricked you", R2tmuzjs_iE [21:47–22:08]).
- **Certainty, then permission:** "You're not supposed to copy this. You're supposed to understand this." (Tr2WIG6-1VU [16:39]).
- **Filler he uses (the seat shouldn't overuse):** "You see where I'm coming from?", "in all honesty", "the interesting thing is", "I'll give you an amazing example", "trust me", heavy swearing, "bro".
- **What he never does on camera:** fake scarcity, discounts, guarantees, persona hooks, leading with AI, partying content for B2B.
- **Seat rule:** describe his thinking in the third person; don't imitate the swearing or the non-native grammar; keep any quote to quotes.md wording.
