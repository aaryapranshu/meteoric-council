## What he checks first
1. What do you sell, at what price, and how many people paid exactly that price?
2. Revenue, cash, profit, AOV; is this the only income? Exact numbers, not "around".
3. Avatar and defined outcome ("freedom" or "10K followers" isn't one).
4. Funnel numbers: spend, visits, qualified followers, conversations, booked, show, close, cost per call, ROAS.
5. Is anything actually broken? "if there's nothing wrong, there's no reason to change anything."
6. Why did past clients pay? (First touch, why you, how sold before the call.)
7. Targeted audience vs clients sold: content problem or marketing problem?
8. Capacity: with unlimited qualified leads, how many clients could delivery take?
9. Founder hours and which are productive.
10. Reorder the founder's list by leverage; one action this week. Detail: `06`.

## Applying this to Meteoric Boost (the brain's application, not something Nik said)
- **Moksh already runs a profile funnel** (content → profile → story sequence → setter → closer). With 139K followers, Nik would skip follower growth and audit conversion: qualified-follower %, per-slide story drop-off, link-sent→booked, show, close, share of buyers pre-sold. Then profile and archived-story ads at $20–150/day, capped near $8–10K/month before retargeting.
- **He'd push price and question the DFY shape.** ~$5–6K for 3 months sold to founders at $50K+/month is cheap by his "what does the problem cost" math. Add a higher level of access, not more deliverables. DFY is the model he left ("done for you offers suck"); it holds only if the Meteoric Engine stays ~80% systematised and responsibility sits with the founder (4–8 camera hours a month is close to his DWY logic).
- **Story sequences as the core client deliverable:** his types, the 6-step slide structure and the ≤5% per-slide drop-off rule fit Meteoric's "7 sequence types"; weight credibility sequences for B2B founders.
- **Sales:** pre-sell 80% so the closer only confirms; a pre-call video between booking and call; score setters on qualified bookings.
- **Messaging:** by his "divided by three" rule, a "$50K/month" floor in copy draws founders nearer $15–20K. Cast the right founders on camera instead ("80% of everything").
