# 01 — Profile funnel, profile ads and story ads

**When to open this file:** "how should we run ads?", "VSL or organic?", "what should a follower / booked call cost?", "our ads stopped working", "how do we track which ad made money?", "should we scale spend?".
- All numbers are self-reported by Nik on camera. Dates matter: his settings and benchmarks drift between 2024 and 2026. Where two versions exist, both are given with the newer one marked.
- Story-sequence mechanics live in `03`. Setter mechanics in `04`.

---

### The profile funnel (his name)
**When to use:** any "how do we get leads from Instagram?" or "should we build a funnel?" question.
**The idea:** Run ads whose only job is to buy qualified profile visits and followers. The existing Instagram profile is the landing page. Reels, stories, highlights, YouTube and proof do the persuasion; a setter starts conversations; a closer (or the DM itself) takes payment. He frames it against "old funnels" that chase leads: here leads come to you. He calls the ads an amplifier of content, never the main driver.
**How to run it:**
1. Fix the profile first: one-line bio with one clear outcome and one CTA ("DM me X"), clear face picture, a credibility pinned post, three highlight topics (story, results, authority). See `02` Profile card.
2. Build the middle: reels on 2–3 core topics, daily story sequences, long-form YouTube linked in bio, a separate testimonial channel or page.
3. Turn on paid traffic to the profile (setup card below).
4. Setter messages new and old followers; sends pain-matched YouTube "assets" before asking for a call (`04`).
5. Pre-sold leads close in DMs or on a short call; non-closers go back into the ecosystem (retargeting, email, stories), not into a "trash can".
**The numbers:**
- Money-printing-machine metaphor: paper = traffic, ink = messaging, toner/heat = conversion system, watermark = proof (t6jzUsmeUTU [03:53]; njWXZNfCm28 [00:00–01:22]).
- Follower count "doesn't matter" (300 vs 100K) for whether it works (QKnBoqLD2Cc [04:30]); a client with 400 followers at $30K/mo (QKnBoqLD2Cc [01:43]); clients with ~1,000 followers at $100–150K/mo (jPGZs6U6CDE [00:40]).
- "$7 million on the profile funnel between me and my clients" (QKnBoqLD2Cc [22:04], Oct 2025); "$6 million" ad spend with clients on it (njWXZNfCm28 [11:42]).
- Durability: if ads pause for a month a VSL business stops; a profile funnel "keeps converting" (rsQQeDm2P0Y [12:02–12:44]).
**Mistakes he calls out:** building a separate landing page and direct funnel when the profile could do it (QKnBoqLD2Cc [04:09]); an unoptimised ecosystem with no clear bio CTA ("you just lost 80% of the conversion", _qpdvJz793E [18:09–19:32]); running only ads because "they're too afraid to solve the actual important part" (YOuvbqU8liU [1:14:03]).
**Ask first:** Does the profile already convert organically (any DMs, any sales from content)? What is the qualified-follower definition, written down? Who replies to new followers within the hour?
**Source:** t6jzUsmeUTU [03:31–05:58], QKnBoqLD2Cc [04:09–05:55], rsQQeDm2P0Y [04:09–07:14], YOuvbqU8liU [1:13:42]

### Marketing does 80% of the conversion, sales does 20% (his recurring rule)
**When to use:** "we need better closers", "close rate is low", "sales calls are long".
**The idea:** In most businesses sales does 70–80% of the convincing. In his, marketing does 80% and the closer answers the last few questions. Direct-response ads flip the ratio the wrong way. If someone needs a long call, the content didn't do its job.
**How to run it:**
1. Ask: what does someone need to have seen to arrive 80% sold? (Usually proof and how you think.)
2. Put that in the profile, stories and YouTube; send it before the call.
3. Keep the call short; treat it as onboarding.
**The numbers:** "my marketing is 80% of the conversion. My sales is 20%" (MyeU41kG1SY [06:07]); direct ads give marketing only 20% (8t92OBM83BA [04:25]); 22% of buyers paid in DMs (wY5TWeKaJow [06:12], Jan 2025); ">35%" of revenue closed in DMs (xTUK3UWMD9A [02:48], Sep 2024); a client running a VSL said 80% of conversion happened on the call (acqxhHB8a5g [30:54]). His closing rate "70 75%" when everything syncs (acqxhHB8a5g [27:51]).
**Mistakes he calls out:** blaming setters and closers when the marketing message attracts the wrong people (njWXZNfCm28 [02:03–02:45]).
**Ask first:** What share of buyers were already decided before the call? What did they consume first?
**Source:** MyeU41kG1SY [06:07], X7pn2j2tK9M [31:38], acqxhHB8a5g [28:12–31:56], 8t92OBM83BA [04:25]

### Profile funnel vs VSL vs webinar vs organic (his comparison table, Sep 2026)
**When to use:** any dispute with a paid-funnel view (Jeremy Haynes seat).
**The idea:** He scores four acquisition models on the same questions and the profile funnel wins most rows. The comparison is his opinion presented on screen, not measured data.
**The rows (his verdicts):**
- Works with zero ad spend? VSL no, webinar no, organic yes, profile funnel yes (slower).
- Minimum spend: VSL $100–300/day; webinar $200–500/day (launch); profile funnel "at least spend $20 per day minimum" (rsQQeDm2P0Y [07:34–08:14]).
- What limits scale: VSL = cost of cold traffic; webinar = show-up and 2-hour retention; organic = posting.
- Cost per lead over time: VSL rises; profile funnel "falls" because credible profiles get followed more cheaply (rsQQeDm2P0Y [09:34–09:55]).
- What you own after a year: VSL "an old video", webinar "old recordings", organic an audience, profile funnel "an audience of buyers" (rsQQeDm2P0Y [11:00]).
- Single point of failure: VSL = ad account (big claims), webinar = attendance, organic = algorithm.
- Will it still work in 3 years? VSL "very unlikely"; webinar likely if done "very very very good" (rsQQeDm2P0Y [14:32]).
**The numbers (other videos):**
- Late-2025 slide: VSL CPC $2–4, CPL ~$30, booked call $250–400, show $300–450; profile funnel $0.20–0.40 per visit, $1–3 per follower, booked call $60–90, show $70–120; "10 to 20x" on a $10K offer vs a VSL "maybe like two 2x" at realistic CAC (t6jzUsmeUTU [11:50–19:40]).
- Jul 2025: $80–100 per qualified booked call vs VSL funnels ~$250–300 (eGWeRi6siNQ [01:40]). Market "250 per book call", Charlie Morgan 250–300 (8t92OBM83BA [06:52]).
- Leon's VSL funnel (client, Sep 2025): 1,662 views → 120 opt-ins → 53 booked → 40 showed → 19 sales (_qpdvJz793E [01:42–03:05]).
**Mistakes he calls out:** "A VSSL funnel is just linear" while the ecosystem is "a web" (_qpdvJz793E [17:49]); "If you turn off ads, you don't make money next day" (QKnBoqLD2Cc [21:03]); long landing pages and 9-minute videos pressure quality buyers (t6jzUsmeUTU [27:21–28:01]).
**Concessions (use these for fairness):** he coached a client's VSL structure (MyeU41kG1SY [59:21–1:01:29]); told Leon to test VSL vs YouTube in bio (_qpdvJz793E [22:55]); told a webinar business doing $402K/mo not to change a working webinar (YOuvbqU8liU [11:01]).
**Ask first:** Is the current funnel actually broken, or just unfashionable? What do you own if ads stop for a month?
**Source:** rsQQeDm2P0Y [07:14–15:15], t6jzUsmeUTU [11:50–20:40], _qpdvJz793E [17:49–19:32]

### Profile-ad campaign setup (UNNAMED; his settings, with drift)
**When to use:** "set up our ads", briefing a media buyer.
**The idea:** One manual Traffic campaign whose conversion location is the Instagram profile, a few ad sets on different audiences, the same creatives cloned across them, then kill and scale on data. He distrusts Meta automation for this.
**How to run it:**
1. Objective Traffic → Instagram profile (never lead forms: "leads always get redirected towards a lead form", eGWeRi6siNQ [20:21]; never Calls, _qpdvJz793E [07:13]). Messenger ads use Engagement.
2. Three ad sets: broad (custom audience excluded) / one interest set (entrepreneurship, lead generation, content marketing, sales, coaching; niche interests for niche offers) / 180-day IG engagers retargeting (eGWeRi6siNQ [05:26–09:57]). Never job titles or schools (eGWeRi6siNQ [06:30]).
3. Advantage+ audience and placements off; multi-advertiser ads off ("Never do a automatic targeted campaign", eGWeRi6siNQ [05:06]; "Never use advantage plus", t6jzUsmeUTU [34:07]).
4. Placements: IG stories, IG reels, IG profile reels only (2025–26) (eGWeRi6siNQ [12:41]; njWXZNfCm28 [14:49]). Dec 2024 version also used feeds and Explore (xcpy678eggY [17:39]): newer view drops them.
5. Demographics: men 20–40 for most of his offers, never 18 (t6jzUsmeUTU [35:32]); adjust to the client's buyer.
6. Test for ~4 days, then cut losers and move budget to the winner; give a winning creative its own ad set.
**The numbers:**
- Budgets: his own ~$150/day per ad set, ~$500/day total (eGWeRi6siNQ [03:43]); minimum $20–25/day (xcpy678eggY [13:07]; t6jzUsmeUTU [33:06]); "If you don't even have 10 bucks a day… you shouldn't be running ads" (njWXZNfCm28 [11:42]).
- Creatives per ad set vary by video: 3 (MyeU41kG1SY [27:41], Sep 2025), 5 (X7pn2j2tK9M [07:12]; _qpdvJz793E [08:39]), "10 to 15" (eGWeRi6siNQ [16:27], Jul 2025).
- Lookalikes conflict: 3–5% B2B and 7–8% B2C (t6jzUsmeUTU [37:55], Oct 2025); 3% B2B and 7–10% B2C (njWXZNfCm28 [13:05], Sep 2025); 0–3% vs 7–10% (_qpdvJz793E [11:21]). Engager window 180 days (eGWeRi6siNQ [08:55]) or 365 if >5,000 followers (t6jzUsmeUTU [37:55]).
- Testing claim: four-day cycles for 40 days and "I can guarantee that you'll find a winning profitable ad" (t6jzUsmeUTU [31:02]).
- A co-host's alternative structure (Jan 2026): test in ABO at ~$50/ad set for 4 days, then move the winning post ID into a broad CBO at ~$250/day; scale ~20%/day (acqxhHB8a5g [13:00–20:16]). Speaker is a co-host, not clearly Nik.
**Mistakes he calls out:** in-app boosting with suggested audiences if your followers aren't already qualified (xcpy678eggY [11:44]; wY5TWeKaJow [10:42]); "only use broad with the most targeted creatives" (_qpdvJz793E [09:20]); tiny audiences (~10K) that cost $400 per booked call (eGWeRi6siNQ [07:13]); judging an ad by cheapest CPC or cost per visit (xcpy678eggY [18:43]).
**Ask first:** What is a qualified follower for this offer? Which organic reels already brought ICP followers? What daily spend can run 30 days without panic?
**Source:** eGWeRi6siNQ [03:43–20:21], t6jzUsmeUTU [31:02–41:01], njWXZNfCm28 [08:58–15:11], xcpy678eggY [11:44–20:44]

### Ad creative: three types, pain-first hooks, two CTAs (UNNAMED)
**When to use:** "write our ads", "which creative should we make?".
**The idea:** Three creative jobs: action-driven (comment/DM a word), problem-solution, and credibility-driven (a client result or status claim in the first 5 seconds). Later videos name educational, credibility and value creatives (a tangible giveaway like a tracking sheet). Hooks trigger a pain, not an identity. The hook only buys the first 3–5 seconds; the body converts.
**How to run it:**
1. Hook (≤4 s): name the pain or a think-number. Not "If you're a coach…" (t6jzUsmeUTU [22:04–23:28]; X7pn2j2tK9M [13:24]).
2. Mechanism: an instead-of-X-we-do-Y contrast (t6jzUsmeUTU [24:11]).
3. Proof: number + timeframe + mechanism ("$900 months to 4.8k months in 60 days", t6jzUsmeUTU [25:17]).
4. CTA: comment-to-get or follow; on video ads not "follow to learn more" (MyeU41kG1SY [30:08]). Long videos get two CTAs (65% and 100% of a VSL; 35% and 100% of YouTube) (eGWeRi6siNQ [28:41]; _qpdvJz793E [22:14]).
5. Same body, new hooks: record ~5–10 variants (7xAo-xh1DwQ [02:45–03:25]).
6. Boost organic reels that brought ICP followers even if they didn't go viral (7xAo-xh1DwQ [08:53]).
**The numbers:** time split hook 5% / body 50% / CTA 10% / credibility 25% / last CTA 10% (X7pn2j2tK9M [17:33]). Viewers either leave in the first 3 seconds or watch the whole ad (jPGZs6U6CDE [06:31]). "over a hundred hooks" tested on one account (wY5TWeKaJow [10:02]). Creative fatigue after 2–3 weeks up to 4–6 weeks; launch replacements a week before (7xAo-xh1DwQ [04:27–04:47]).
**Conflict:** Jun 2025 he says the first 3 seconds should be "the most controversial" thing for that ICP (jPGZs6U6CDE [10:36]); Sep 2025 he says "don't focus on controversy ego. Focus on make people think" (MyeU41kG1SY [1:01:29]). Newer view: make them think. Odd numbers ("say 11, say 9, say 13") beat round ones (eGWeRi6siNQ [25:51–26:53]).
**Mistakes he calls out:** lifestyle-only creatives bring cheap, unqualified leads (acqxhHB8a5g [17:52], co-host); third-party name-drops as hooks without proof (a client's angle of having paid a well-known company's sales chief) read as hype (jPGZs6U6CDE [08:54–10:16]); production quality as "the least important one" is his organic rule (MIPEikK6H3s [12:41]), but "lighting is super important" for ads (X7pn2j2tK9M [11:02]).
**Ask first:** What are the top three pains in the founder's DMs this month? Which reel brought the most qualified followers?
**Source:** xcpy678eggY [01:43–07:11], t6jzUsmeUTU [20:40–28:01], njWXZNfCm28 [03:28–04:09], X7pn2j2tK9M [13:24–18:57]

### Story ads and archived-story ads (UNNAMED)
**When to use:** cheap creative, warm audiences, B2B high ticket.
**The idea:** "nobody is running story ads". Take past stories from the archive, add "follow to learn more" or a short text claim, run them as story-placement ads. They act as instant retargeting and face-frequency. Next step: pay for people to see a full story sequence as a carousel.
**How to run it:** archive → pick past stories with a result or claim → add text that names the outcome (bare Stripe screenshots pull unqualified followers) → run in the story placement → test text-heavy vs screenshot vs single CTA.
**The numbers:** ~$350 over 3 days → 126 followers, "cost per qualified follower for three bucks" (eGWeRi6siNQ [18:34]); "over 5,000 stories" archived (_qpdvJz793E [14:48]); "almost $1 million around 8 to 900" spent with clients on story ads in 4–5 months (njWXZNfCm28 [11:42], Sep 2025); a client's £330 on story ads → 2 PIF sales (eGWeRi6siNQ [00:20]).
**Mistakes he calls out:** a bare result screenshot with no context; "less followers but more qualified" once text is added (X7pn2j2tK9M [08:56–10:20]).
**Ask first:** How many stories are in the founder's archive with a real result in them?
**Source:** t6jzUsmeUTU [13:32–15:54], eGWeRi6siNQ [16:49–19:21], _qpdvJz793E [14:48–17:29], 7xAo-xh1DwQ [07:50]

### Benchmarks he gives for the profile funnel
**When to use:** "are our numbers good?".
**The idea:** He judges the funnel per stage, and qualified follower and cost per qualified booked call matter more than cost per visit.
**The numbers (his, high-ticket B2B to info/consulting buyers):**
- Jul 2025 targets: qualified follower under $13–14; ≥80% of new qualified followers get a conversation; qualified share ~30% (under 20% = creative problem); $80–100 per qualified booked call; show ≥75%; ROAS 8–15x (eGWeRi6siNQ [00:40–02:00]).
- Real month, Jan 2025: $12,825 spend, $0.23/visit, 14% visit→follower, ~$5 per qualified follower, $7 per conversation, 146 booked (≈$83 each), 123 qualified (≈$99), 101 shows (≈$120 each), $151K cash, "12.4x" (wY5TWeKaJow [06:32–08:17]).
- Real month, Oct 2025 (1–27th): $17K spend, 32K visits, 4,618 followers (14.2%), 2,292 qualified, 1,576 qualified conversations, 134 booked, 109 shows (~81%), $241K cash (QKnBoqLD2Cc [09:43–11:48]).
- Real month, Aug 2026: ~$38K spend, 8,000+ followers, "less than $5" per qualified follower, $457K, "12x" (rsQQeDm2P0Y [04:29–05:11]).
- Oct 2025 slide: B2C $0.25/visit, $1–3/follower, $3–10/qualified follower, $6–20/conversation, $40–100/booked call, ~50% close; B2B $0.50/visit, $2–6/follower, $5–20/qualified follower (t6jzUsmeUTU [41:01–42:04]).
- B2C info (Aug 2025): $0.03–0.15/visit, $1–5/follower, $2–10/qualified follower, $5–15/qualified conversation, $10–50/call (X7pn2j2tK9M [00:00]).
- Messenger ads: under $15/message B2C, under $30 B2B (MyeU41kG1SY [43:54]).
- Cost per booked call for his clients at $500K–1M/mo: "40 bucks up to 120 bucks max" (acqxhHB8a5g [33:18]).
**Mistakes he calls out:** cutting the $200/booking ad that actually produces cash in favour of the $150 one: "you want to know what ad makes you money not what ad books you appointments" (wY5TWeKaJow [08:38–09:00]).
**Ask first:** What is your cost per qualified booked call and cash per call, not cost per visit?
**Source:** as listed

### Spend cap and scaling rules (UNNAMED)
**When to use:** "should we double the budget?".
**The idea:** Scale on ROAS after ~30 days, fix the funnel before spending more, and accept that profile-visit ads plateau.
**How to run it (Oct 2025 rules):** under 2x → turn off; 2–4x → fix setter, closer and profile first; 4–10x → double spend; 10x+ → triple, and "make sure you have the sales team ready" (t6jzUsmeUTU [43:07–44:10]). Sep 2025 version: above 10x ×3, above 4x ×2, 3–4x means other components are broken (njWXZNfCm28 [18:36–18:56]).
**The numbers:** profile-visit ads plateau after ~$200–300/day; cap them at ~$8–10K/month, then add story ads, retargeting and asset funnels (eGWeRi6siNQ [02:41–03:22], Jul 2025). "When you increase ad spend, you slowly decrease conversion" (QKnBoqLD2Cc [11:28]). Note the Aug 2026 month ran ~$38K, so his own cap has moved or includes other ad types.
**Mistakes he calls out:** changing ads when the problem is the middle of the funnel, or the reverse (17Oc1LRuRKI [07:59]).
**Ask first:** Is ROAS measured on cash collected, over at least 30 days?
**Source:** t6jzUsmeUTU [43:07], njWXZNfCm28 [18:15–18:56], eGWeRi6siNQ [02:41]

### Attribution: which ad made the follower (his manual method)
**When to use:** "we can't track follower ads".
**The idea:** Follower ads are hard to attribute, so the setter traces each new follower to its source and logs it. He later automated this in his software.
**How to run it:**
1. Instagram notifications → Follows → tap the follower → "from your ad" → see the creative (works when ads run from an account connected to the Instagram profile) (17Oc1LRuRKI [09:01–10:24]; 7xAo-xh1DwQ [02:03]).
2. Do the same for organic reels to learn which hooks bring ICP followers.
3. In Ads Manager add the Results column; review weekly (njWXZNfCm28 [18:15]).
4. Onboarding form for every new client: first touch point, why you (`04`).
**The numbers:** tracking sheet columns: ad spend, followers, cost per follower, DMs, cost per DM, Calendly invitations, calls booked, cost per call, show rate, sales, close rate, CPA, cash, installments, ROAS (17Oc1LRuRKI [07:17]).
**Ask first:** Can the setter name, today, which ad produced last week's buyers?
**Source:** 17Oc1LRuRKI [06:55–11:04], 7xAo-xh1DwQ [02:03–02:45], eGWeRi6siNQ [14:24–15:26]

### Retargeting and reactivation instead of a "trash can" (UNNAMED)
**When to use:** "what do we do with leads who didn't book or didn't close?".
**The idea:** Every non-buyer goes back into the ecosystem: retargeting ads, email, follow-ups, and a one-button booking page.
**The numbers:** retargeting "only 30 bucks a day" plus setter plus nurture = "our biggest return"; "booking two calls a day from this on autopilot", target five (QKnBoqLD2Cc [20:36–23:46]). His own revenue never swung 50K→150K because he always has ads and new creatives running (7xAo-xh1DwQ [03:46–04:27]).
**Source:** QKnBoqLD2Cc [20:36–23:46], eGWeRi6siNQ [38:34–39:34], CAxLCrjz5Ss [22:50]
